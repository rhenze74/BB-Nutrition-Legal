Privacy Policy

What this app stores, where it's kept, and the few times anything leaves your iPhone. The app has no server and no accounts.

01Overview

There's no server, so we have nothing to collect

Buzzer Beater Nutrition tracks what you eat, your weight and your body measurements. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. Everything you enter is kept in a private database on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us, and we never see it.

02What's stored

Only what you enter

CategoryExamples
ProfileFirst name, sex, birth year, height, units, activity level and weight goal — used only to estimate your calorie needs
Food logFoods you log, amounts, meals, times and their nutrients; water
Your foods & recipesFoods you create or scan, favorites, recipes and their ingredients and steps, and a recipe's web address if you imported it

The food database built into the app (from the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central) ships inside the app; searching it happens on your iPhone.

03Where it lives

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage

Your data is stored with Apple's SwiftData framework in storage only this app can read, encrypted by your device passcode like any other app's data.

Backups

Once a day the app saves a backup file in its own folder on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › BB Nutrition › Backups) and keeps the newest 14. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on for your iPhone, these are included like any app's data — that backup belongs to your Apple account and we have no access to it. Export a Backup creates a file you choose where to save or send; the app doesn't upload it anywhere.

04Apple Health

Only if you connect it, only what you allow

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you do, and only for the types you allow in the Health permission sheet:

  • Saved to Health: the food you log (calories, macronutrients, vitamins and minerals), water, weigh-ins, waist measurements and body fat.
  • Read from Health: weight, waist and body fat (for example from a smart scale or Buzzer Beater Fitness), and active and resting energy, shown next to your calorie estimate.

Health data stays in Apple Health on your device under Apple's privacy terms. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone. You can change what the app may read or write any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › BB Nutrition.

05When data leaves your iPhone

Three lookups you start, and an update check

Barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode the app doesn't already know, it sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to get the product's nutrition. Nothing about you is sent. Open Food Facts receives the request like any website visit, under its own privacy policy.

Recipe imports. When you paste a link and tap Import, the app downloads that one page from that website to read the recipe — the same as opening it in a browser. The website sees an ordinary page request. Nothing else is sent.

Update check. About once a day the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup which version is current. The request contains only the app's identifier and your device's region.

That's all. Nothing you log, no profile details and no Health data are ever included in any of these.

06Camera & microphone

Used only when you ask, processed on your iPhone

Camera: used to scan barcodes and photograph nutrition labels. Label text is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework; photos aren't saved by the app or sent anywhere. Choosing a photo from your library shares only that photo with the app.

Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you tap Say It to describe a meal. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone — the app requires on-device recognition, so your voice is never sent to Apple's servers or to us. The text is split into foods on your iPhone (using Apple's on-device model where available).

07What we don't do

No tracking, no ads, no selling data

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of any kind
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information. We never have it.
08Your control

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry, food, recipe or weigh-in removes it from your iPhone (and its copy in Apple Health, if the app saved one). Settings › Backups & Data › Delete All Data erases everything after a two-step confirmation. Data already in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Deleting the app removes all of its data and backups from your iPhone; files you exported are kept wherever you saved them.

09Not medical advice

A tracking tool, not a doctor

Calorie estimates and targets come from standard formulas and your own data, and food values are approximate. They aren't medical or dietary advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are changing your diet significantly, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian.

10Children

Not directed at children

Buzzer Beater Nutrition is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from children — as described above, it doesn't collect information from anyone.

11Changes

If anything changes, this page changes first

If a future version sends data off your device in a new way — for example sharing recipes publicly or estimating calories from a photo with an online service — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the release notes will say so.
